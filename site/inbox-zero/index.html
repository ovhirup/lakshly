01 / Rescue

Start with unread.

Mark unread inbox messages as read. Already-read mail stays outside Rescue, so you can focus on the backlog.

02 / Review

Find what deserves you.

Review sender, subject and preview snippets in a focused sprint. Importance suggestions use rules and your local feedback; you make the call.

03 / Garden

Make progress feel good.

See your review progress grow in your Garden. Choose to share an aggregate Inbox Wrapped recap without sharing mailbox content.

Noise Radar

Check the noise.
Keep the choice.

Scan both read and unread inbox messages for possible spam, and review marketing senders. Scanning alone does not move emails or change their read status.

Suggestions can be wrong. Review the evidence before choosing a mailbox action; this is not a security verdict.
