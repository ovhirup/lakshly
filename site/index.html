Questions, answered

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. Statements are read and categorised on your device. In the web app, a Web Worker in your browser does it, and nothing is uploaded. If you later turn on Premium sync, it’s end-to-end encrypted, so it’s unreadable to us.

Do I have to link my bank account?

No. Lakshly doesn’t ask for bank logins. You import the statement PDFs or CSVs you already get, and your mutual fund CAS from CAMS/KFintech. You can also connect Gmail read-only (web beta) to find statements; it’s read from your browser, never via our servers.

If I connect Gmail, does Lakshly see my email?

No. Gmail is read directly from your browser by Google’s API, never via our servers. Lakshly only searches a fixed list of bank, card and CAS senders, opens just their statement attachments on your device, keeps the read-only access in memory, and shows every email it read in a log. Disconnect revokes access in one tap. (Web beta, invited testers first.)

Which banks and cards are supported?

Today: CAMS/KFintech detailed CAS, HDFC, SBI and ICICI account statements, and HDFC and SBI Card statements. Other banks use a generic reader, and more adapters are on the way. Scanned (image-only) PDFs aren’t supported yet.

My statement PDF has a password. Is that okay?

Yes. Lakshly asks for it, uses it once to open the file on your device, and never stores it.

What does Premium cost?

₹119/month or ₹999/year in India, and $4.99/month or $39.99/year elsewhere. The free tier stays free, and security features are never paywalled.

Does this page track me?

No. There are no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. If you followed a link with campaign tags (like utm_campaign=lakshly-launch), those tags are sent with your waitlist sign-up only, so we know which post you came from. Nothing is stored in your browser.

Is Lakshly open source?

Yes. The app code is AGPL-3.0-or-later. The shared schema, parsers and synthetic demo data are MIT. Browse the code on GitHub.
