Waitlist privacy notice

Plain-language notice under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

Your email address. If you arrived through a link with campaign tags (for example, a link in our launch post on X that ends in ?utm_source=x&utm_campaign=lakshly-launch), those tags are saved with your sign-up, so we know which post or site you came from. That’s all: no cookies, analytics, device fingerprinting or third-party scripts on this site, and the tags aren’t stored in your browser or sent anywhere unless you submit the form.

Why (purpose)

Only to email you about Lakshly’s launch and product updates. We never use your data for advertising, and never sell or share it for marketing.

Your consent and choices

  • We add you only if you tick the consent box. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Buttondown uses double opt-in: after you sign up you’ll get an email asking you to confirm, and you’re only added once you click the link.
  • You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email, or by writing to hello@lakshly.com.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or erase your data. We delete it when you unsubscribe or when the waitlist ends, whichever comes first.

Where it’s stored

Waitlist emails are stored by Buttondown (see Buttondown’s privacy policy at buttondown.com/legal/privacy), the email service we use to run the waitlist. Buttondown is our data processor: it stores your email address (and any campaign tags from the link you followed) and sends our emails, only on our instructions. When you submit the form, your browser sends it directly to Buttondown and opens Buttondown’s confirmation page in a new tab. This site itself stores nothing.

The Lakshly app: Gmail connect and Sign in with Google (beta)

In the Lakshly web app beta you can optionally Sign in with Google and connect Gmail (read-only) to find your bank, card and CAS statements.

  • Gmail is read directly from your browser by Google’s API, never via our servers. Lakshly has no server in the path: your browser gets a short-lived read-only access token from Google, keeps it in memory only (never stored, never sent to us), and calls the Gmail API itself.
  • Only emails from a fixed list of finance senders (banks, card issuers, CAMS/KFintech/NSDL/CDSL) are searched, and only their statement attachments are opened. Everything else in your inbox is never searched or read.
  • Statements are read on your device, like a file you import yourself. Raw emails and attachments are not kept; the imported transactions stay encrypted in your browser.
  • Sign in with Google asks only for your name and email address. They are read in your browser to fill in your name and inbox and are not sent to Lakshly.
  • You can see every email Lakshly read in the in-app read log, and Disconnect revokes access at Google in one tap. You can also remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Lakshly’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Lakshly is an independent open-source project. For privacy requests (access, correction, erasure, withdrawing consent) or grievances, email hello@lakshly.com. Please don’t post personal data in public GitHub issues.
