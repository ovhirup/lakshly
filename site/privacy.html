Waitlist privacy notice

Plain-language notice under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

Your email address, plus your optional answer to “What would you track first?”. Nothing else: no cookies, analytics, device fingerprinting or third-party scripts on this site.

Why (purpose)

Only to email you about Lakshly’s launch and occasional product updates. Your optional answer is used, in aggregate, to decide what to build first. We never use your data for advertising, and never sell or share it for marketing.

Your consent and choices

  • We add you only if you tick the consent box. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also open an issue on GitHub (without your email) and we’ll reply with a private way to reach us.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or erase your data. We delete it when you unsubscribe or when the waitlist ends, whichever comes first.

Where it’s stored

With the email provider we use to run the waitlist, which processes it only on our instructions. We’ll name the provider here before the waitlist goes live.

Contact

Lakshly is an independent open-source project. For privacy requests or grievances, use the contact that will be listed here at launch, or open an issue on GitHub (please don’t post personal data there).
